What it does

Four things, done carefully.

01

A local-first archive

Sessions from Claude Code, Codex and Gemini CLI are copied, never moved or symlinked, into ~/AgentHome. When an agent clears its own logs after 30 days, your history is still here. Sync is live and incremental: new messages show up seconds after they are written.

~/.claude · ~/.codex · ~/.gemini → ~/AgentHome

02

Costs that add up

Token usage is priced with the LiteLLM price table, with cache reads and cache writes billed at their own rates. Checked against ccusage, totals land within about 1%. Break it down by day, model, project or session.

≈1% vs ccusage

03

Quota at a glance

Remaining subscription quota and reset countdowns sit on one dashboard. Claude quota comes from the official claude /usage; Codex from its own rollout snapshots. Run low and the resident in the cabin gets tired.

claude /usage · codex rollout

04

Find any line with ⌘K

Global full-text search across every session, Chinese included. Matches are highlighted in context, and picking one jumps straight to the original message in its session.

⌘K

Privacy & compliance

Your sessions stay on your Mac.

AgentHome is a local app with no account and no server. It only reads what your agents already wrote to disk.

Local-first
The archive, index and settings live under ~/AgentHome on your machine.
Read-only sources
Agent directories are read and copied. AgentHome never edits, moves or symlinks them.
No credentials
It does not read tokens, API keys or login files. Claude quota is read by running the official claude /usage.
No telemetry
No analytics, no usage reporting. The source is open for you to check.

Install

Three steps, about a minute.

  1. 1

    Download

    Get AgentHome-0.2.6-macos-arm64.zip from the latest GitHub release.

  2. 2

    Move to Applications

    Unzip and drag AgentHome.app into Applications. It is signed with a Developer ID and notarized by Apple.

  3. 3

    Open it

    On first launch it builds your archive from the sessions already on disk, then keeps watching for new ones from the menu bar.
